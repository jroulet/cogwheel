- **VALIDATED: the interior coordinate is the MERGING-PAIR DELAY, generated by
  an ODE** `[→ spec]` — owner's construction, measured 2026-08-06 at
  `gamma = 0.495`.

  Inside the astroid there are 4 images; approaching a fold one PAIR coalesces,
  so that pair's delay difference `d_tau -> 0` at the caustic. The envelope
  oscillates as `exp(i w d_tau)` for exactly that pair, so LEVEL SETS OF
  `d_tau` are the surfaces along which the demodulated envelope is smooth.
  The transverse coordinate is arc length along those contours.

  ## The construction

  Work in ONE quadrant. The shear matrix is diagonal, so reflections about
  both axes generate the other three exactly, and `d_tau` is EVEN across a
  cusp axis (reflection maps the merging pair to its mirror) — there is no
  chart boundary there and nothing to track on "the other side".

  Integrate `dy/ds = J grad(d_tau) / |grad(d_tau)|` (RK4, `J` = 90 deg
  rotation) from a seed on the hard axis until the walk reaches the soft axis.
  Step the seed outward and repeat: contours are the radial coordinate,
  arc-length fraction along each is the transverse one.

  All 12 seeded contours run hard-axis to soft-axis and conserve `d_tau`. The
  DEEP INTERIOR is the best-conserved region; error grows toward the caustic
  only because `d_tau -> 0` there (relative error) and coalescing images make
  branch matching harder.

  Also measured: `d_tau^(2/3)` is proportional to perpendicular
  distance-to-caustic to **2%** (ratio 1.207-1.235 over `r` = 0.30..0.93),
  confirming the fold law `d_tau ~ s^(3/2)`. So `d_tau^(2/3)` is the
  correctly-stretched radial variable and a UNIFORM node grid in it is already
  well spaced.

  ## The pair MUST be carried by continuation

  Selecting the merging pair by `argmin` of image-plane separation at every
  evaluation FAILS deep inside: there the runner-up pair is tied to ~0.2%
  (`sep2/sep1 ~ 1.002`, versus 3-5.6 near the caustic). The pair LABEL stays
  stable at the sample points and `d_tau` drifts CONTINUOUSLY — so this is not
  relabelling at the samples. It is the finite-difference GRADIENT: at
  `h = 2e-4` the stencil straddles the pair-switching surface, contaminating
  `grad(d_tau)` slightly at every step, which accumulates into smooth drift
  (spread ~1, i.e. 100%).

  Fix: choose the pair ONCE at the seed and carry it by matching images to the
  previous point's images by proximity, so `d_tau` is a single analytic branch
  — including inside the `+-h` stencil, which is where the contamination
  entered. That single change took the inner contours from 100% drift to
  5e-10.

  ## Why the shipped wedge coordinate fails

  `r = |y| / r_caustic(theta)` is NOT this coordinate. `d_tau` varies by
  1.3-3.5x ALONG a contour of constant wedge-`r`, so the residual phase
  `w * d_tau` still oscillates along the chart's own coordinate surfaces —
  which is what the spline then has to resolve. (Gradient MISALIGNMENT is
  small, median 3.1 deg, max 21.4 — so the failure is the level-set LABELLING,
  not gross misorientation.)

  - Cost note: `d_tau` needs the quartic image solve per table point —
    semi-analytic and fine for building a table, too slow per query, which is
    exactly why it must BE a table.
  - Validate against the retired `ffin` baseline (106/106 charts at median
    3.42e-4), not merely against the 5e-2 bar.
