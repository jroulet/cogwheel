KUMMER REPARAMETRIZATION (the brief's item 1, and the reason the ladder is cheap): reparametrize so that a' = iw/2 is K-INDEPENDENT. This is a STRUCTURAL win — it makes the ladder terms share a numerator — NOT a precision win. Do NOT claim Kummer buys precision: z = -i*w*s/2 is purely imaginary so |e^z| = 1 and Kummer moves no magnitude; both forms have identical max term e^{w*Y}.

WHERE DD IS MANDATORY (quantitative): the Maclaurin series in z — BOTH the terms and their summation. Compensated/Kahan summation does NOT rescue this: its bound still carries sum|term_i|, and the terms are exponentially larger than the result (max term ~ e^{w*Y} at k* = w*Y/2). Float64 (eps=2.2e-16) crosses the 1e-10 gate at L_1F1 = w*|y'| = 13, and the 2-image regime at Y>=0.5, w=40 exceeds that — this is the null-hypothesis bulk of a microlensing posterior, not a corner case. Also dd the Pochhammer/base^k ladder that builds G^(k), since it feeds the Maclaurin at the same precision.

WHERE FLOAT64 IS PROVABLY FINE: the prefactor C(w) = exp[pi*w/4 + (i*w/2)*ln(w/2)]*Gamma(1 - i*w/2). It is a COMMON multiplicative factor across all k and all n, so its relative error factors out of the sum exactly and contributes ~1e-16 flat, unamplified. Do NOT dd it. TWO MANDATORY TRAPS: (a) exp(pi*w/4) OVERFLOWS at w~900 while Gamma(1 - i*w/2) underflows like e^{-pi*w/4} — compute via complex loggamma and combine exponents ANALYTICALLY; never evaluate exp(...)*gamma(...) as written. Implement the closed-form overflow-free magnitude |C(w)|^2 = pi*w/(1 - e^{-pi*w}) using expm1 for the small-w limb (from Gamma(1+ix)*Gamma(1-ix) = pi*x/sinh(pi*x)). (b) The (w/2)*ln(w/2) phase cancels against Im loggamma(1 - i*w/2) — cancel it ANALYTICALLY, not numerically.

SHARED-NUMERATOR LADDER, NO K-RECURRENCE, NO LARGE-|z| BRANCH (the brief is explicit): do NOT k-ladder the 1F1 itself via contiguous relations. Forward recurrence for 1F1(a+k; 1+k; z) is frequently UNSTABLE — the minimal solution gets swamped. The Kummer reparametrization's k-independent a' is what lets the ladder share a numerator instead. Evaluation count must scale O(max_order), not O(max_order^2) — assert that.

EXPONENT-RANGE CAVEAT: dd extends the MANTISSA, not the EXPONENT. hi is a float64, so overflow is still 1.8e308 and the low word is silently lost near denormals. |G^(k)| reaches ~1e92 at w=40, k=84 and OVERFLOWS at w ~ 700 with max_order=42. Gate the input w with a clear named error above the certified ceiling rather than returning silent garbage.

numba-SHAPED, DEFER @njit: plain loops, plain arrays, no closures, no containers, homogeneous scalar returns, no object mode. Document the intended hot path in the module docstring. Add @numba.njit(cache=True) only if WP3's performance gate fails without it.

mpmath IN TESTS ONLY — never import it from cogwheel/lensing/.
