### Serving micro-levers: faster geometry, parallel exact evaluation, tabulated Pearcey

The serving path sheds its two dominant residual costs via
profile-first, value-preserving optimizations; the exact Schwinger
evaluation parallelizes across frequency nodes (bit-exact per node,
with refusal semantics unchanged and scheduling-independent),
multiplying training-campaign throughput by roughly the core count;
the Pearcey special function is served from a precomputed,
hash-verified table with a certified live-quadrature fallback; and
the geometric branch gains runtime image-count and Morse parity
guards, with its handoff threshold provably bracketed rather than
historically trusted.
