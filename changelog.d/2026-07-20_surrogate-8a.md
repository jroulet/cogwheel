### A surrogate learns the envelope

The amplification engine gains an offline-trained surrogate: a tensor
cubic-spline emulator of the beat-free SACR-C envelope over a
four-dimensional lens-parameter space (the shear-frame rotation
removes the orientation angle exactly, and lens mass and redshift
enter only through the dimensionless frequency).  It is an additive
speed layer, off by default: the exact engine remains the ground
truth and the fallback, every named refusal is preserved by a
conservative domain gate that never answers where the engine would
refuse, and enabling it by default awaits the full-box training
artifact and production-scale validation.
