### Uniform-asymptotic serving closes the last fast-serving holes

Two certified uniform evaluators join the serving ladder: a fold
(Airy) arm built from finite fold-frame curvatures with its prefactor
pinned by the exact geometric limit, and a cusp (Pearcey) arm whose
Pearcey primitive is evaluated by rotated-contour quadrature and
certified by paired resolution before any physics prefactor. Both
self-certify per node against the envelope accuracy bars and fall
through — never serve — when uncertifiable. The high-frequency corner
that homogenization left as a named refusal is now served wherever
the arms certify, and the surrogate charts' cusp exclusion windows
shrink to the arms' certified coverage at runtime. Previously
certified geometric and exact paths are byte-identical.
