### Added: Born weak-deflection rung (dormant, not wired)

`cogwheel/lensing/chang_refsdal/_born.py` provides the analytic Born
amplification for the low-`w` far zone: `born_amplification`, the validity gate
`born_gate` with its named refusal `BornDomainError`, and `born_envelope`, which
demodulates in the partition's min-relative-delay frame using the shared
`switched_analytic_channels` primitive rather than introducing a delay-frame
convention of its own.

The series is expanded about `sqrt(mu_macro) = 1/sqrt((1-kappa)**2 - gamma**2)`,
not about 1, and its correction carries `w` in the numerator so it vanishes as
`w -> 0` and recovers the macro-magnification limit. Positive parity only: the
convergence radius is the parity wall, so macro-saddle draws refuse by name.

The rung is NOT wired into `_surrogate_coefficients`. Its O(1) coefficient `b1`
is an unpinned placeholder and the amplification disagrees with `operator.F_op`
by up to ~13% inside the region where `born_gate` passes, so the annulus
`3.0 < |y| <= 4.2426` continues to fall through to the exact engine. Wiring
requires a closed form for `b1` and an oracle-accuracy gate at a stated
tolerance.
