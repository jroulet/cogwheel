### Global multi-chart lens-amplification surrogate: charts, trainer, census, registration

The Build-8a single-box surrogate becomes a global, multi-chart,
first-class data product. Near-caustic tube charts in caustic-adapted
coordinates (gamma, u = sqrt(eta), theta, log w) — the caustic sits at
the fixed plane eta = 0 and u = sqrt(eta) absorbs the fold branch —
plus raw-coordinate far-field charts per image-count region, selected
at query time by a deterministic guard stack keyed only on certified
quantities (caustic distance, image count; theta solely for
cusp-window exclusion). Cusp neighborhoods are excluded and served
exact. An offline resumable training driver reads the prior box from
the prior classes, derives cusp structure from the geometry engine
with topology cross-checks, and bisects gamma into topology-stable
sub-bands. A census tool reports served fraction with a five-way
fall-through breakdown, held-out envelope accuracy, lnL accuracy
tiers, and a measured relative-binning-floor line. The artifact is
registered end to end (DATA_CONTRACTS, data registry, consumer-graph
loader). Off by default: every path is byte-identical until a trained
artifact is explicitly passed; the full-box training campaign is
deferred until the engine and chart set are final.
