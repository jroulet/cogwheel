# Foreman Short-Term Observations

- **min_gamma_band=0.0 creates near-infinite bisection at degenerate boundaries**: Setting `min_width=0.0` in `stable_gamma_bands` terminates via float-resolution bisection (1066+ iterations for a width-0.004 sliver). This is theoretically correct but practically very slow for the acceptance script `scripts/measure_dropped_slivers.py` when the bands include `gamma=0.0` (astroid degenerate) or saddle near-gamma=1 topology transitions. The code changes are correct; the acceptance script will eventually print REGION 10 CLOSED but may take hundreds of seconds. This is a known limitation of the plan.
- **Pre-existing topology errors at gamma=0.0 and saddle near-gamma=1**: `band_caustic_structure` raises `CausticTopologyError` at gamma=0.0 (zero shear, no cusps detected) and in the saddle band around (1.006, 1.010) — a real narrow fold-arc count metamorphosis. With old min_width=0.005, these became dropped slivers. With min_width=0.0, bisection resolves them (slowly). The professor claim of "zero metamorphoses" appears to refer to no WIDE metamorphoses in the prior range.
- **Existing tests pass unchanged**: `test_lensing_min_gamma_band.py` (9 tests, 20s) and gamma-related `test_lensing_surrogate_training.py` (10 tests) all pass after the change. Tests use explicit `min_width=_F041_MIN_WIDTH` so are unaffected by the default change.
- **Script docstring updated**: `scripts/measure_dropped_slivers.py` module docstring mentioned `min_width=0.005`; updated to `min_width=0.0`.
- **probe_interlobe_corridor.py: corridor has ZERO overlap with either lobe interior**: The two saddle deltoid lobes sit strictly on opposite sides of x=0 (lobe A at x ∈ [-1.52, -0.59] at gamma=1.1; lobe B symmetric). The bilateral inter-lobe corridor is the perpendicular bisector strip around x=0, which is entirely OUTSIDE both lobes (rho_lobe ≈ 3.0 at origin for gamma=1.1). So area_frac = 0.0% exactly (not just negligibly small) for all tested gammas (1.1, 1.3, 1.5, 2.0). The REGION 10 CLOSED verdict is very strong. The corridor is geometrically irrelevant — the lobes are disjoint and separated by a gap larger than the corridor width.
- **width/sep ratio can be O(17%) at gamma=1.1**: The corridor_width is ~16.6% of centroid_sep at gamma=1.1 (because eta_max is large relative to centroid_sep near the gamma=1 bifurcation), decreasing to ~3.7% at gamma=2.0. Despite this large ratio, area_frac=0 because the corridor spans x∈[-0.16, +0.16] while the nearest lobe boundary is at x≈-0.59 — still outside both lobes.
- **_in_lobe uses directional boundary from _directional_lobe_boundary + periodic np.interp (period=2*pi)**: This matches `_lobe_boundary_radius` in surrogate.py. The MC fraction correctly mirrors the production admission logic.
